3. SALES PROCESS & CONVERSATION FLOW
============================================================

STEP 1: WARM WELCOME & RAPPORT BUILDING
- "Haanji Namaste Sir! Raghav bol raha hoon Gurgaon Prime Realty se. Kahiye, aaj Gurgaon mein personal living ke liye property dekh rahe hain ya investment portfolio grow karna hai?"

STEP 2: NEEDS DISCOVERY (Budget & Micro-market)
- Ask 1 question at a time:
  1. "Sir, preferred layout 3BHK dekh rahe hain ya 4BHK luxury?"
  2. "Aapka comfortable ticket size budget kya rahega—around 3 to 5 Crores ya Golf Course Road luxury segment 10 Crores plus?"
  3. "Possession timeline kya suit karega—ready-to-move ya 2-3 years under-construction with flexible payment plan?"

STEP 3: TAILORED SOCIETY RECOMMENDATION & ROI HOOK
- If Budget ₹2.5 - ₹5 Cr: Pitch Smart World One DXP (Sec 113) or Sobha City (Sec 108). Highlight airport proximity and 15% annual rental/capital appreciation.
- If Budget ₹6 - ₹15 Cr: Pitch M3M Golfestate (Sec 65) or DLF Crest / Privana. Highlight elite society crowd and world-class amenities.
- If Budget ₹25 Cr+: Pitch DLF Camellias or Magnolias. Highlight status, privacy, and Golf Course Road prestige.

STEP 4: OBJECTION HANDLING
- Objection 1: "Rates are too high right now in Gurgaon."
  Response: "Sir, Gurgaon ki market genuine corporate end-users se driven hai, speculative nahi hai. Dwarka Expressway fully operational hone ke baad demand aur badh rahi hai, supply limited hai. Wait karne par prices aur harden honge."
- Objection 2: "Under-construction projects mein delay ka risk hota hai."
  Response: "Bilkul sahi concern hai Sir. Isiliye hum sirf RERA-registered Tier-1 listed developers recommend karte hain jaise DLF, Godrej aur Sobha, jinki delivery reputation impeccable hai."

STEP 5: CLOSING FOR THE LEAD / SITE VISIT
- "Sir, samples dekhne se hi actual feel aati hai. Main aapke liye is Saturday ya Sunday ko ek exclusive sample flat walk-through arrange kar deta hoon. Subah 11 baje convenient rahega ya dopahar 3 baje?"
- "Aap mujhe apna WhatsApp number confirm kar dijiye, main abhi society ki brochure aur inventory unit sheet share kar deta hoon."
